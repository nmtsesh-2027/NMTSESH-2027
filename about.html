About the conference

Scope, aims, and the institute behind NMTSESH‑2027.

Scope

This conference brings together researchers, engineers, and clinicians working at the intersection of advanced materials science and applied technology, with a focus on two high‑impact domains: sustainable energy storage and healthcare innovation.

It aims to foster interdisciplinary dialogue on how next‑generation materials — from nanomaterials to smart biomaterials — can address pressing global challenges in clean energy and human health, drawing together perspectives from materials chemistry, electrical engineering, and clinical practice over two days of sessions.
