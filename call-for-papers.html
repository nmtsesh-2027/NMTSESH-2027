Call for papers

Submit your research to NMTSESH‑2027

Original, unpublished work is invited across eight themes spanning materials science, energy storage, and healthcare technologies. Submissions are collected through a simple Google Form.

How submission works

Authors submit their manuscript details and paper through the NMTSESH‑2027 Google Form. The organising committee reviews submissions and reaches out by email with next steps.

Before you submit

  • Prepare your manuscript following the conference formatting template.
  • Have your paper ready as a PDF, or as a shared Google Drive link with view access enabled.
  • Identify which of the eight themes your work fits best — you'll select this on the form.

Submit via Google Form

Fill in your author and paper details, attach your manuscript, and submit — you'll receive an email confirmation once it's received.

Open submission form
  1. Open the NMTSESH‑2027 submission form
  2. Fill in author names, affiliation, and contact email
  3. Select the theme your paper best fits
  4. Attach your manuscript or paste a Drive link to it
  5. Submit — a confirmation email follows automatically
