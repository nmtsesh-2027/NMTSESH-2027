NMTSESH-2027

7–8 January 2027 · National Institute of Technology Puducherry, Karaikal

Next‑Generation Materials and Technologies for Sustainable Energy Storage and Healthcare

NMTSESH‑2027 brings together researchers, engineers, and clinicians working at the intersection of advanced materials science and applied technology — from nanomaterials to smart biomaterials — to address global challenges in clean energy and human health.

"Fostering interdisciplinary dialogue on how next‑generation materials — from nanomaterials to smart biomaterials — can address pressing global challenges in clean energy and human health."

NMTSESH‑2027 is organised by the Department of Chemistry and the Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering at NIT Puducherry, Karaikal, under the patronage of the Institute's Director. The conference spans eight major themes, from advanced functional materials through to computational materials discovery and technology‑assisted healthcare.
